1. Who we are

Known Good Media ([legal business name and entity type, e.g. "Known Good Media LLC"], "we", "us") makes highlight and recruiting videos for student athletes. This policy covers our website, knowngoodmedia.com, our parent portal, and the services we provide when you order a reel. Questions about it go to hello@knowngoodmedia.com or [mailing address].

2. Our services are for parents and guardians

Many of the athletes we make reels for are under 18, and some are under 13. Our website and parent portal are meant for parents, legal guardians and adult athletes, not for children. A parent or guardian places the order and provides the athlete's information, and by doing so confirms that they are the athlete's parent or legal guardian (or the athlete is 18 or older) and agree to this policy on the athlete's behalf.

We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information without a parent's involvement, contact us and we will delete it.

Coaches and team parents can order team or youth reels. If you order for a team, you confirm that you have permission from each athlete's parent or guardian to share their film and information with us.
